01 / RESEARCH DIRECTION

Ground performance,
from test to model.

My work examines how soil properties and ground improvement methods influence strength, compressibility, and settlement. I combine laboratory characterization with numerical analysis to study solutions for soft and variable ground.

Sand compaction piles, prefabricated vertical drains, deep cement mixing, and soil improvement for embankments and transit infrastructure.

MASTER’S RESEARCH

Comparing soil models for embankment settlement
